About This Species

Scientific Name: Phaeognathus hubrichti

Status: Endangered

Habitat: Deep moist ravines in AL Red Hills region. (Only 6 counties!)

Diet Ants, beetles, small invertabrates

Size: 6-10 in

This is the only salamander species in its genus to be endemic to Alabama
